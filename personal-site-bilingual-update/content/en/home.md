Hello, and welcome to my personal website.

With a background in physics, I am interested in theoretical neuroscience and its connections to artificial intelligence. Through mathematical modeling and computation, I hope to understand how neural systems learn, form memories, and process information.

This website brings together my research interests, study notes, reflections on research, and moments from everyday life.

## Research Interests

- **Neural dynamics:** continuous attractors, network dynamics, and population activity.
- **Learning and memory:** representational drift, continual learning, and memory stability.
- **Brain-inspired artificial intelligence:** biologically plausible learning mechanisms and neural decoding.
