I am interested in neural dynamics, learning, and representations, and in how these mechanisms can inform artificial intelligence.

## Current Research Interests

The following are topics I am currently exploring. Details of specific research projects will be added as they develop.

### Neural Dynamics and Continuous Attractors

How network structure shapes population activity, and how continuous attractors support the encoding and maintenance of information.

### Representational Drift and Continual Learning

How neural representations change over time, and how changing neural activity relates to stable memories and behavior.

### Brain-Inspired Learning and Neural Decoding

Biologically plausible learning rules and the application of machine learning to decoding neural signals.
